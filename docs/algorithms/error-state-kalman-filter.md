# Error-state Kalman filter (eskf)

**What it does:** estimates a body's motion from a 6-axis IMU (accelerometer and gyro): attitude, velocity and position in the world (z up), together with the gyro's and the accelerometer's biases, each with its uncertainty. It is the joint estimate that [[mahony-ahrs]] plus [[strapdown-inertial-navigation]] approximate with separate heuristics, in which one estimate can absorb another's signal: a rest test against the moving gyro bias learns a slow turn as bias, and the attitude carried on that gyro then leaks gravity, so a slow rotation drifts to one side. Here the gyro bias is a slow random walk (0.2 mrad/s per √s): following a 0.13 rad/s turn would take its σ days. Generic for any IMU.

**Inputs → outputs:** `New(DefaultConfig())` returns a filter waiting for its first rest; `Prior(&fit)` seeds the biases from a multi-pose calibration ([[startup-calibration]], `calib.Solver`'s `Fit`) whose `Apply` the caller runs on the accelerometer's samples. `Update(dt, acc, gyro)` takes the specific force (m/s², +g up at rest) and the rate (rad/s), sensor frame. After each call: `Q` the attitude [w,x,y,z] (sensor to world), `V`, `P` the world velocity and position, `GyroBias`, `AccelBias` (sensor frame), `Ready` (the first rest has set the tilt; nothing moves before), `Still` (quiet gyro and steady force), `Calm` (not translating). `Sigma()`: the attitude, velocity and position σ. `Reset()` forgets the motion, keeps the biases.

**Method:**

- Nominal state propagated per sample (quaternion, world acceleration, v, p). Error state δθ (world), δv, δp, δb_g, δb_a: 15. Covariance first order and sparse: P += dt(AP + PAᵀ) + Q dt with A's four nonzero blocks (A[θ,bg] = −R, A[v,θ] = −[a_w + g ẑ]×, A[v,ba] = −R, A[p,v] = I).
- Updates are sequential scalar ones, each optionally gated on its innovation (χ², 1 dof), accumulated and injected once; then `guard` keeps the float32 covariance usable: a positive diagonal and each correlation within ±1 (without it P − k·phᵀ cancelled to rounding and squares closed metres away). That is all it holds: the update is not in Joseph form and a pairwise clamp doesn't make 15 states positive semidefinite. Over ten-minute runs the smallest eigenvalue of the correlation matrix reaches −0.2, and −2.6 in some uncalibrated ones, in directions no measurement touches: over about 1.8 million scalar updates the innovation variance never fell below the measurement noise, and the estimate stayed finite.
- **Tilt** (every `TiltEvery` samples): the force turned into the world against ẑ, two components, with the accelerometer bias in H. Gated unless calm: right after a rest the attitude is known to a fraction of a degree, so a stroke's horizontal acceleration is an outlier and the gyro carries the attitude through it (Mahony's fixed gain can't: it leans into every acceleration).
- **Looking still** (zero velocity may begin): *calm*, the world acceleration low-passed over 0.1 s under `StillAccel` for `CalmTime` (a rotation in place passes; the force alone can't tell: a horizontal acceleration changes |f| by a²/2g, and a smooth stroke passes a steadiness test), or a *long rest*, quiet with the force on a gyro-turned reference (a fixed world vector seen from the turning body) for `RestTime`, even when the world acceleration says otherwise (an unseen offset tilts it over the limit after a turn). A fast stroke's middle is calm only for a moment; a slow one's is not: 3 cm over 2 s peaks at 0.043 m/s² and stays under the limit throughout, at up to 0.028 m/s.
- **A rest begins** as soon as the body looks still, unless after a translation (the force left its turned reference) its speed is over `MoveSpeed`: a stroke's middle, while a pause's velocity error stays under it. After a turn it doesn't ask the speed (an unseen offset's leak runs it up), nor after `MaxGlide` looking still.
- **The shadow** ends a rest that is a slow stroke: the body's acceleration over the last second in 0.1 s slots, untouched by zero velocity, its velocity and displacement from rest at the window's start. Gravity is taken off in the body frame by a reference turned with the gyro (with its bias over 10 s, snapped to the estimate when that moves 2 mrad/s: the estimate wanders as a hand's tremor passes into it) and seeded at each rest from the force's gyro-turned 0.15 s average (a plain average lags a turning body). Taken off in the world instead, a 0.1° attitude error leaves g·δθ, as much as a 3 cm stroke's acceleration. Over `StillSpeed` it wakes the rest and gives back its velocity and displacement; then the body glides, and rest returns once the speed has stayed under `StillSpeed` for `StillTime`. It decides only while the body is quieter than that, 3× its rms at rest (learnt from the shadow of a second ago, so a stroke can't raise it before it wakes) under `StillSpeed`: a steady hand's tremor moves it as fast as a 3 cm stroke, so in a hand slow strokes are left to zero velocity as before; on a desk they register.
- **Zero velocity** gated, so a velocity the covariance says is real is kept, except while the body is still (quiet gyro, steady force: rest by its own evidence; gated there, a velocity grown from an unseen offset's leak was refused, the leak ran on and the speed sawed between 0 and 0.15 m/s after an uncalibrated turn); refused for half a second, it is taken with the velocity's variance reopened to its own size (a gate must not lock an error out).
- **Zero rate** (still: quiet gyro, steady force): the mean rate over 0.1 s, its noise the window's own spread (a hand's tremor is real rotation), gated so a slow turn stays out of the bias; an axis refused for a second reopens the bias's variance to the refused innovation (a warming gyro; about the vertical only the zero rate sees it).
- **First rest:** quiet and steady for `StillTime`, then `AvgTime` of force averaged: tilt from it, yaw 0.

**The honest bound:** there is no position fix. An acceleration error δa (a tilt error δθ gives g·δθ) moves the position by ½·δa·t² until zero velocity: 0.1° is 1.9 cm over a 1.5 s stroke and 0.85 m over 10 s of motion without a pause. Paused shapes (strokes, squares with held corners) can be true; a long continuous motion's absolute path can't, for any consumer IMU. Uncalibrated, an accelerometer offset can't be told from tilt until a turn: a 45° turn with MPU6050-sized offsets (0.2–0.3 m/s²) moves 0.3–1.3 m (calibrated: ≤ 6 cm). A turn barely teaches the offset either: after 10 s still and a 90° turn the estimate is still up to 0.058 m/s² off a 0.04–0.06 m/s² offset.

**Known limit: a shaky hand at rest, uncalibrated.** Held still for 60 s in a shaky hand (an 8 Hz translational tremor of 0.09 m/s², rotational 0.027 rad/s; 10 seeds) the position drifts up to 96 cm with a multi-pose calibration's prior and up to about 35 m without one. The tremor keeps the body from looking still most of the time (calm about 15 %, against 80 % for the attitude-free still test), and uncalibrated the accelerometer's offset leaks into the world acceleration as well, which defeats any rest test built on it. The remedy is the calibration (`Prior`); a steady hand stays put (0.06 cm over 20 s).

**Input contract:** finite samples at a steady rate (`dt` a sensor period, 0.01 s at 100 Hz). `Update` doesn't check, and one bad sample is not forgiven: a NaN makes the state NaN, or before the first rest keeps the filter from starting, until `Reset`; a 1 s `dt` leaves a 32–70° tilt error; a saturated accelerometer sample 6–9° for a minute; a saturated gyro sample (34 rad/s) a 26–35° error, 105° after 3 minutes, with the gyro bias estimate corrupted to 0.06–0.08 rad/s, which `Reset` keeps, so the gyro never looks quiet again and the filter never becomes `Ready`: only `New` recovers. Drop or clip such samples before `Update`.

## Completeness review

- Complete: no. No application runs it yet.
- Tested (`eskf_test.go`, a hand model fitted to a recorded hand-held MPU6050: rotational tremor the accelerometer sees, an 8 Hz translational one, white 0.016 m/s² and 0.0006 rad/s, offsets (0.04, −0.06, 0.05) m/s² unless calibrated, gyro bias (10, −6, 8) mrad/s):
  - **Slow rotation and drawn shapes:** `TestSlowTurnIsATurn` (20°/2 s, 20°/5 s, 45°/5 s about each axis, held 10 s: tilt ≤ 0.25°, moved ≤ 6 cm, gyro bias ≤ 4 mrad/s), `TestEarlySlowTurnIsNotBias` (a turn right after power-on, the bias still uncertain), `TestSquaresClose` (15 and 30 cm, seeds 7–10: inside the box + 25 %, closure ≤ 30 % of the side; measured 2–10 % and 29 % once), `TestStrokeRegisters` (in a hand, calibrated, 40 seeds × 2 directions: 10 cm in 1 s within 10 % in 61 of 80; 5 and 3 cm over 2 s within 0.5–1.5× in only 16 and 9 of 80: a slow stroke's middle is as slow as the tremor and goes to zero velocity), `TestSlowStrokesOnADesk` (uncalibrated, offsets and gyro bias still being learnt, 40 seeds × right and up: 3, 5 and 10 cm over 2 s within 0.5–1.5× in 64, 73 and 80 of 80; 0 for 3 and 5 cm without the shadow on seeds 7–9). Both assert floors a few strokes under the measured rates.
  - **Biases and rests:** `TestFirstRestSetsTheTilt` (four poses, ≤ 0.2° of the hand's true attitude), `TestFirstRestWaitsForATurnToEnd`, `TestRestLearnsTheGyroBias` (desk 0.5 mrad/s, steady hand 10 mrad/s in 5 s: what its tremor averages down to), `TestRestFollowsABiasChange` (a 20 mrad/s step, each axis, in 5 s), `TestUncalibratedTurnComesToRest` (MPU6050-sized offsets: stopped, ≤ 0.05 m/s, and ≤ 1.5 m after a 45° turn and 10 s held; over 30 such turns, three axes, with and without tremor, the speed peaks at 0.013 m/s in the last second and the body moves at most 62 cm).
  - **Consistency and numerics:** `TestSigmaIsHonestAtRest` (50 seeds: the mean normalised error squared at rest inside its χ² 95 % band, tilt 1.68 in 1.45–2.55 and velocity 2.94 in 2.32–3.68; after a turn bounded, see Consistency), `TestTenMinutesStayAFilter` (finite, positive diagonal, correlations within ±1), `TestGuardKeepsTheCovarianceUsable` (a negative variance and a correlation of 10 are floored and clamped by the next injection), `TestUpdateDoesNotAllocate`, `TestNewRejectsConfig`.
- Correct: yes on these properties. 14 mutants of the rest decision, each failing a test: no give-back on waking, no quiet gate, no `MoveSpeed` gate, no translation flag, no seed at a rest, no bias snap, no glide, no `StillTime` before a glide rests, the floor learnt from the current shadow, the reference not turned, no reference follower, no turn bypass, no `MaxGlide`, zero velocity gated while still. Of the first design's 8 mutants, 6 still fail a test: zero rate ungated, no covariance guard, the rest test ignoring the gyro, calm from the force instead of the world acceleration, no long rest, zero rate never reopened. A separate pass of 58 mutants on the update paths (propagate, predict, scalar, tilt, zero velocity, zero rate, inject, guard, wake, start): 45 fail a test. Untested (they survive every test): the tilt update's gate (never gated, or always) and the bias subtracted in it: in the tested scenarios the gyro carries the attitude and the tilt's gain after a rest is small; zero velocity forced after half a second refused, its variance reopen and the refusal timer; zero rate's white-noise floor; the sequential innovation (the tilt's two components share the bias columns). Near-equivalent: the ½·a·dt² term in P, the symmetrise step (`guard` copies the upper triangle), `normalise` in `inject` (`predict` normalises every sample), zero velocity never gated (a real velocity while calm and not still doesn't occur). Not reached by a test or an application's motion tests: a real acceleration ending a glide (without it, a stroke after a glide would rest only under `StillSpeed`). Removed after their mutants survived every test and an application's motion tests: the first rest's gravity-magnitude bias along up; a wrong-rest give-back (a rest begun in a slow stroke's middle); the shadow ignored after a turn; a minimum shadow to give back.
- Consistency: σ is honest at rest (50 seeds: tilt 1.68 against 2, velocity 2.94 against 3; the gyro bias 3.82, just over its band) and too small after a turn: tilt 7.26 against 2 (σ about half the error's spread), velocity 4.65 against 3, gyro bias 6.16 against 3. Uncalibrated, a horizontal offset and a tilt are one unobservable direction and the tilt σ collapses (normalised ~200): the long rest and zero velocity ungated while still keep that from locking the filter out; a multi-pose calibration's prior is the remedy.
- Margin: `StillSpeed` 0.02 m/s is a knife edge for any one stroke: on one seed only 0.020 registered every case (0.019 left the slow strokes to zero velocity, 0.021 read a 3 cm stroke as 4.64 cm). Over 40 seeds the rates move smoothly and trade a desk's slow strokes against a hand's: a 3 cm desk stroke registers in 64–69 of 80 between 0.018 and 0.020, 52 at 0.022 and 6 at 0.025; in a steady hand slow strokes mostly don't at any setting (3 cm: 0 of 80 at 0.016, at most 12 up to 0.025; 5 cm: 3 to 27), because a hand's shadow at rest (p90 0.019 m/s) is as fast as a 3 cm stroke's middle (0.028).
- Speed: ~190–230 ns per `Update` on the host (Apple M5, `BenchmarkUpdate`, depending on the machine's load), 0 allocs. On the Tufty 2040 (`cmd/kernelbench`, TinyGo, 0 B heap): 1.307 ms per `Update` moving and 2.592 ms at rest (13 % and 26 % of a 100 Hz sample).
- TinyGo 0.43 `-target=tufty2040` (`cmd/kernelbench`): 119,500 B flash; `-print-allocs=eskf` lists only `New`'s 2488-byte `Filter`.
