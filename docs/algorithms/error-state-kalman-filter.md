# Error-state Kalman filter (eskf)

**What it does:** estimates a body's motion from a 6-axis IMU (accelerometer and gyro): attitude, velocity and position in the world (z up), with the gyro's bias and the accelerometer's (the calibration's), each with its uncertainty. It is a motion estimator and nothing else: dead reckoning with a Kalman filter. Nothing decides whether the body rests, turns or glides, and nothing outside is fed back into it. It is an `estimator.Motion` (`x/math/nav/estimator`: `Update`, `Attitude`, `Ready`, `Velocity`, `Position`), and it sits in a pipeline of single-responsibility stages: sensor (raw, calibration applied) → filter (signal conditioning, if any) → this estimator; whatever decides on its estimate is a separate stage. Generic for any IMU.

**Where:** `x/math/nav/eskf/`, one responsibility per type:

- `eskf.go`: `Config` (`valid` the one place a configuration is checked), `Filter` (:98: composes the parts below), `Init` (:124: in place), `New` (:142), `Prior` (:162), `SetRange` (:184), `Reset` (:199), `Attitude`, `Velocity`, `Position`, `Ready` (:206–215: `estimator.Motion`), `Update` (:231: validate, then start, or predict and every `TiltEvery` samples tiltUpdate and inject), `validate` (:257: finiteness and clips, the counters), `tiltDue` (:270), `start` (:285: the first attitude by `estimator.Level`), `predict` (:317), `tiltUpdate` (:332: applies the tilt model), `inject` (:347), `Sigma` (:357);
- `state.go`: `State` (:9: the nominal state, embedded in `Filter`), `turn` (:50: the attitude by a rate, an exact rotation), `move` (:72: the force into the world, velocity and position), `correct` (:86: the error injected);
- `covariance.go`: `covariance` (:7), `propagate` (:39: Φ·P·Φᵀ + Q dt), `row` (:104), `update` (:116: one scalar measurement, consider states, Joseph form), `guard` (:155);
- `tilt.go`: `tiltConsider` (:10), `tiltModel` (:30: the measurement's rows, innovations and noise), `motionWindow` (:66), `motionPower` (:68: the force's recent variance, the body's acceleration);
- `input.go`: `inputs` (:8: the clip limits through the fit), `clipped` (:48), `finiteSample` (:59).

**Inputs → outputs:** `Init(&f, c)` sets a filter in place, allocating nothing and building no second `Filter` (on a microcontroller one returned by value is a second 2.9 KB block); it also restarts a used filter. `New(c)` returns one by value. `Config.Gravity` is local gravity as the calibrated accelerometer reads it: the calibration's measured |g| (the mean corrected |f| at rest; `StandardGravity` without one). `Prior(&fit, σ)`, before the first `Update` (or after `Reset`), takes the biases from a multi-pose calibration ([[startup-calibration]], `calib.Solver`'s `Fit`) whose `Apply` the caller runs on the accelerometer's samples; the accelerometer's bias stays the calibration's (zero after its `Apply`), its σ the largest of σ, the fit's RMS and 0.01 m/s² (a NaN or infinite σ or RMS adds nothing). `Update(dt, acc, gyro) error` takes the specific force (m/s², +g up at rest) and the rate (rad/s), sensor frame; `estimator.ErrSample` for a refused sample. After each call: `Q` the attitude [w,x,y,z] (sensor to world), `V`, `P` the world velocity and position, `GyroBias`, `AccelBias` (sensor frame); `Attitude()`, `Velocity()`, `Position()` return them, `Ready()` is true once the first sample has set the tilt. `Rejected` and `Clipped` count the samples refused and those with a reading at a full scale. `SetRange(accel, gyro)` changes the full scales in place. `Sigma()`: the attitude's σ about the world axes (att[0] and att[1] the tilt about world x and y, att[2] the yaw), the velocity's and the position's. `Reset()` forgets the motion and returns the biases to the prior.

**Method:**

- **Start** (`estimator.Level`): the first accepted sample sets roll and pitch from its force (yaw 0), the attitude's variance TiltNoise² + ((|f| − g)/g)², the yaw's 1 rad², the velocity's 0.1 m/s, the position at the origin.
- **Prediction** per sample (`State.turn`, `State.move`, `covariance.propagate`). The attitude turns by an exact rotation (q ⊗ [cos(θ/2), sin(θ/2)·ω/|ω|]) at the gyro's rate averaged with the previous sample's (a gyro reads the rate at the sample); the sample's force is turned into the world with the attitude at the step's end (with the step's start, a turn in place with exact samples moved 2–17 cm). Error state δθ (world), δv, δp, δb_g, δb_a: 15. Covariance stepped as Φ·P·Φᵀ + Q dt with Φ = I + A·dt (to first order alone P lost definiteness over ten-minute runs); the velocity and position variances inflated by ×(1 + 1e-5) a sample against float32 rounding in long motion.
- **Tilt** (`tiltModel`, every `TiltEvery` samples): the force turned into the world against ẑ, two components. It corrects the attitude only: the accelerometer's bias is the calibration's, since gravity's direction can't tell a bias from a horizontal acceleration (estimated from it, a hand's strokes and a shake moved the bias by up to 1 m/s² and the attitude settled 4–7° off the force at rest). The velocity, the position and the accelerometer bias are consider states in it (Schmidt, Joseph form): gravity's direction says nothing of the first two (corrected through the correlation, one update moved the position by 9.9 m with exact samples), and the bias stays the calibration's with its columns in H (a bias error tilts the measured force by b/g): the covariance then knows that a residual bias tilts the estimate by b/g rather than drifting the velocity. Without those columns the velocity's σ grew as σ_b·t (30–90× its error on a still sensor) and the tilt's σ fell 2.5× short of the error under a real residual; with them, against truth drawn from the filter's own priors, each error over its σ stays within [1/3, 3] (`TestSigmaHonestAgainstThePriors`). Its noise, never gated and never switched: TiltNoise² (0.014 rad: with the bias's tilt in the covariance, 0.01 read a 5 cm stroke 3 s after the start up to 16 mm off), ((|f| − g)/g)² (an acceleration along gravity), and the body's own acceleration, which across gravity barely shows in |f|: the force's variance over the last half second above the sensor's white noise (`motionPower`, 3·AccelNoise²/dt subtracted) times `LinAccelTime` (96 s), as a white-equivalent density over the span since the last update. On a desk that term is zero and the tilt corrects the attitude quickly; while the body moves it lengthens the tilt correction's time scale well past a stroke, so the gyro carries the attitude and a stroke's acceleration and deceleration cancel. The window: 1 s left the attitude 0.56° off the force 2 s after a set-down (at a scale of 16 s); 0.25 s dropped a slow stroke's own acceleration between its peaks (3 cm over 2 s read 57 %). The scale: from 8 s to 96 s the conformance suite's 3 cm stroke over 2 s along world x (its one noise draw) grows from 71 % to 80.5 % (98 % along y); at 192 s a slow turn's tilt reaches 0.26°.
- **Updates** (`covariance.update`) are sequential scalar ones, accumulated and injected once (`State.correct`); then `guard` keeps the float32 covariance usable.
- **Clipped readings** (`inputs.clipped`): a clipped gyro loses the step's rotation (skipped, the attitude's variance grown by (GyroRange·dt)²); a clipped accelerometer alone keeps the gyro's rotation (propagated with the clipped force, the velocity's variance grown by (AccelRange·dt)², no tilt). Skipping such samples whole lost the rotation with the covariance unaware: after a shake the attitude was 80° off at σ 0.2°.

**The honest bound:** there is no position measurement. An acceleration error δa (a tilt error δθ gives g·δθ) moves the position by ½·δa·t², smoothly and without bound. On a recorded ±8 g badge capture of strokes and holds (calibrated, `Gravity` its measured 9.8129 m/s²; 104 s) the position is 0.11 m off after 5 s, 0.32 m after 10 s, 0.75 m after 20 s, 13 m after 40 s and 23 m at the end (21 m horizontally, 8.5 m down); with the standard 9.80665, 1.43 m after 20 s and 33 m at the end (the 7.5 mm/s² difference alone drifts 1.2 m vertically in the first 20 s). Measured from each stroke's start, the strokes' sizes are off by a median of 346 % (the drift velocity at their starts). Plain integration of the same samples (the gyro's attitude, the fit's biases, no measurement) ends 1,184 m off.

**Known limits:** a small slow stroke is measured to within a few millimetres, not to a fraction of its size: its own force raises the tilt's noise, so the drift is corrected differently than in the same run without it. Over eight noise draws a 3 cm stroke over 2 s is off by 0–41 % (median 13 %; the error as often across the stroke as along it, for a vertical stroke too), 2 of 8 draws over 20 % on each axis; 10 cm ones are within 1–6 % at the suite's draw. A filter 3 s old can't tell a 3 cm creep over 2 s from a lean: off by up to 22 mm (13–22 mm on the three axes over eight noise draws), where 13 s after the start it is within 15 mm. A steady sideways push (constant acceleration) has no variance once the window has passed it, so the tilt then reads it as a lean. Uncalibrated, the tilt 20 s after a shake is 0.4–0.6° off (calibrated 0.01–0.10°).

**Input contract:** finite samples at a steady rate (`dt` a sensor period, 0.01 s at 100 Hz). `Update` refuses a sample with a NaN or an infinity, or a `dt` not above zero (`Rejected`), without touching the state. The full scales (`AccelRange`, `GyroRange`: by default ±2 g and ±2000 °/s; `SetRange` for another range; after `Prior` the accelerometer's is taken through the fit) decide `Clipped`. A gap is integrated as one step: after one, call `Reset`.

## Completeness review

- Complete: no. No application runs it yet.
- Tested (`eskf_test.go`, a hand model fitted to a recorded hand-held MPU6050; `estimatortest.Motion` with a calibration's prior): `TestConformance`, `TestItOnlyIntegrates`, `TestNoPositionStep`, `TestTiltUpdateLeavesVelocityAndPosition`, `TestAccelBiasIsTheCalibrations` (5 s of ±2 m/s² sideways pushes while turning: the bias unchanged bit for bit, the tilt within 0.5° at rest after), `TestSigmaHonestAgainstThePriors` (40 draws with the truth from the priors: tilt and velocity error over σ within [1/3, 3] at 10 and 40 s), `TestQuaternionStaysNormalised`, `TestFirstSampleSetsTheTilt`, `TestTurnInPlaceStaysPut` (≤ 1 mm over the turn; measured 0.01–0.23 mm), `TestSlowTurnIsATurn` (tilt ≤ 0.25°, measured ≤ 0.21°; gyro bias ≤ 4 mrad/s, measured ≤ 0.24), `TestTiltRecoversAfterShaking` (≤ 0.5° 20 s after; measured 0.01–0.10°), `TestSigmaIsNotTooSmall`, `TestResetStartsAgain`, `TestDeterministicReplay`, `TestInit`, the covariance's definiteness and guard, clips, non-finite samples, `Prior`, `SetRange`, `TestUpdateDoesNotAllocate`, `TestNewRejectsConfig`, `TestTenMinutesStayAFilter`.
- Binding estimator gates (a hand rig and the recorded ±8 g capture, calibrated): translation response 18 of 18 within 20 %; attitude at rest after motion 0.01–0.03° from the force (bar 0.5°), and within bar on a second capture's desk; tilt error at most 0.21 σ (bar 3 σ).
- Correct: yes on these properties.
- Consistency: σ is conservative, never too small: the mean normalised error squared at rest is tilt 0.01, velocity 0.04, gyro bias under 0.01 (a steady hand's tremor counts as the body's acceleration, and nothing observes the velocity).
- Speed: on the host (Apple M5, `cmd/kernelbench`, a busy machine) about 300–450 ns per `Update`; 0 allocs. TinyGo 0.43 `-target=tufty2040` (`cmd/kernelbench`): no allocation listed in the package. Not yet timed on the Tufty 2040.
