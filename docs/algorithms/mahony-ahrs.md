# Mahony attitude filter (brick)

**What it does:** estimates a body's attitude from a 6-axis IMU. The gyro's rate turns the attitude; the accelerometer's direction, taken as gravity's, pulls the tilt towards it through the error e = a × v (measured against estimated gravity), proportionally (Kp) and, optionally, through an integral ∫e dt that learns a gyro bias (Ki). Without a magnetometer the heading is the gyro's alone; `UpdateMag` adds one, which pulls the heading as gravity pulls the tilt. It is an `estimator.Attitude` brick: the same shape as [[error-state-kalman-filter]] (an `estimator.Motion`) without velocity and position, so either serves a caller that needs only the attitude. It is EasyRobot's one Mahony filter (it was `x/math/filter/ahrs.MahonyAHRS`, moved here): plain arrays instead of matrices, an in-place `Init`, the gains in a `Config` checked once, the first sample levelling the attitude, and the integral taken per second (the old filter added e per sample, so its Ki depended on the sample rate: its 0.005 at 100 Hz is 0.5/s² here). The roll, pitch and yaw of its [w,x,y,z] output: `ahrs.Euler` (x/math/filter/ahrs/euler.go).

**Where:** `x/math/nav/mahony/mahony.go`: `ErrConfig` (:23), `Config` (:26), `DefaultConfig` (:32: the badge's Kp 2, no integral), `valid` (:34: the one place the gains are checked), `Filter` (:40), `Init` (:56), `New` (:67), `Reset` (:75), `Attitude` (:78), `Ready` (:81), `Update` (:90: 6-axis), `UpdateMag` (:109: 9-axis), `level` (:125: the first sample), `mid` (:133: the step's rate, the mean of the two samples'), `stepFloat` (:139: the float 6-axis update), `corrected` (:148) and `correctedMag` (:170: the feedback), `turn` (:209: the exact rotation); `float.go` (`step`, the default float 6-axis update) and `fixed.go` (`-tags fixedmotion`: `step` in fixed point, Q30 attitude and integral, Q22 accelerometer, Q24 gyro, Q28 gains, a 128-bit product for the integral term). The shape: `x/math/nav/estimator/estimator.go`: `ErrSample`, `Attitude`, `Motion`, `Level` (the attitude whose up is a force, heading 0). The conformance suite every brick runs from its own tests: `x/math/nav/estimator/estimatortest` (`Attitude(t, ctor)`, `Motion(t, ctor)`).

**Inputs → outputs:** `Init(&f, mahony.DefaultConfig())` (in place, no allocation; `New` returns one by value); `Update(dt, acc, gyro) error`: the specific force (m/s²) and the rate (rad/s), sensor frame; `UpdateMag(dt, acc, gyro, mag) error` with a magnetometer's reading (any unit). A NaN or an infinity, or a `dt` not above zero, is refused with `estimator.ErrSample`, counted in `Rejected`, the attitude untouched. `Attitude()`: the quaternion [w,x,y,z], sensor to world (z up); `Ready()`: the first accepted sample has levelled it. `Reset()` starts it again.

**Method:** the first accepted sample sets roll and pitch from its force (`estimator.Level`, heading 0). Each later one: a = acc/|acc|; v = the world's ẑ seen in the sensor frame from q; e = a × v (plus, with a magnetometer, the measured field's direction against the one q predicts from the field's own horizontal part and dip); ∫e += e·dt when Ki > 0; ω = ḡ + Kp·e + Ki·∫e, where ḡ is the mean of the last sample's rate and this one's (a gyro reads the rate at the sample: the step's rate is the trapezoid's, the convention [[error-state-kalman-filter]]'s prediction uses, so the bricks agree; the newer sample's rate alone would lead by half a sample, 0.5 % of a steadily accelerating turn at 100 Hz); q ← q ⊗ [cos(θ/2), sin(θ/2)·ω/|ω|] with θ = |ω|·dt, normalised against rounding: an exact rotation (axis-angle), as eskf's, not the published filter's first-order step q ⊗ [1, ω·dt/2], which turns by 2·atan(θ/2), short by θ³/12 (0.75 % of a 30 rad/s spin at 100 Hz; the fixed-point path takes cos and sin/θ of the half-angle by their series to its sixth power, exact to rounding while |ω|·dt/2 ≤ 0.65 rad: the rate's magnitude up to 130 rad/s at 100 Hz, which a ±2000 °/s gyro (34.9 rad/s per axis, 60.5 on a diagonal) stays inside; above it the series falls short, a 1 s turn at 173 rad/s 1.1e-3 rad off and at 222 rad/s 9.5e-3 rad). A zero force gives no correction. An acceleration is read as tilt: at Kp 2 the tilt follows it with a time constant of about 0.5 s. With `-tags fixedmotion` the 6-axis step runs in integers (the RP2040's Cortex-M0+ has no FPU), within 1e-4 rad of the float path (its rotation exact while |ω|·dt/2 ≤ 0.65 rad); the 9-axis update is float either way.

- Complete: no. The badge's app moves to this brick from `ahrs.MahonyAHRS`.
- Tested (`mahony_test.go`): `TestConformance` (`estimatortest.Attitude`), `TestMatchesReference` (one step against the published filter, integral per second, the rotation exact, 6- and 9-axis), `TestRampedRateTurnsExactly` (a linearly ramping rate turns by its exact integral: the step's rate the mean of the two samples'), `TestFastSpinTurnsExactly` (20 rad/s for 1 s within 1e-4 rad: the rotation exact), `TestConvergesToAStaticTilt` (6- and 9-axis, from a level start), `TestCancelsAGyroBias` (6- and 9-axis, 10 minutes), `TestIntegralTakesOutAGyroBias`, `TestAccelEdgeCases`, `TestInitRejectsConfig`, `TestDoesNotAllocate`; `fixed_test.go` (`-tags fixedmotion`): `TestFixedFollowsFloat` (rest in five tilts, spins to ±6 rad/s, hand motion, zero accelerometer samples, integral gains; Kp 2 and 10), `TestFixedInitForgets`; `estimator_test.go`: `TestLevel`, `TestLevelIsUnitNearUpsideDown` (|q| within 1e-6 of 1 as the force nears upside down). The feedback's sign reversed (v × a) fails the conformance suite and the bias test; the normalisation dropped fails the unit-quaternion test.
- Correct: yes on these properties.
