# Strapdown integrator with zero-velocity updates (strapdown)

**What it does:** gives the translation half of a body's 3D path from an IMU:

- the accelerometer's specific force is rotated into the world by an attitude;
- gravity is removed;
- the rest is integrated into velocity and position.

Until its first rest the attitude is an attitude filter's; from then on the Nav carries its own on the gyro and levels it to gravity at rest. Built for the Tufty badge (ER-067): its Motion page shows the path, and Pong moves the paddle with the badge's vertical motion. It is generic for any IMU.

**Where:** `x/math/nav/strapdown/strapdown.go`:

**Attitude source (`Config.ExternalAttitude`).** By default the Nav takes the filter's attitude until its first rest, then carries its own on the gyro and levels it to gravity only at rest. A filter leans into slow strokes; the gyro doesn't. But the MPU6050's gyro bias wanders: in a recorded hand-held capture it changed by 0.0007 rad/s between two still stretches (76–88 s and 89–114 s), so between rests the Nav's own attitude drifts about 1° per 25 s. On a still desk with false wakes it reached 1.39°, which reads as 0.26 m/s² of fake acceleration.
- `ExternalAttitude: true` takes the supplied `q` on every sample and skips the levelling at rest (`:446`, `:967`). The filter's levelling time constant (Mahony's 1/Kp) then sets the trade between drift and stroke lean, which is the caller's choice (picked from a τ table). Default false: unchanged.
- Measured through the badge app (an RP2040 badge demo with the attitude-source tests, Mahony Kp 2, τ 0.5 s, a recorded hand-held capture and its sensor model):

| | own attitude (default) | `ExternalAttitude` |
|---|---|---|
| Attitude on the desk (bar ≤ 0.5°) | 1.39° | 1.10° |
| desk display wander | 199.8 cm | 11.2 cm |
| 10 cm horizontal stroke from rest (bar 2 cells) | passes | 0.36 cells (the filter absorbs it as tilt: A5) |

- Tests: `TestExternalAttitudeIsTheSuppliedOne` (Q is the supplied attitude from power-on, through the first rest, a 2 s rest and a biased turn; the accelerometer's horizontal offsets stay in Bias and A is nil, so neither rest levelled the attitude; the default moves them into the attitude and leaves Q), `TestExternalAttitudeStrokeTracks` (10 cm up in 1 s reads 10 cm and rests).

**First rest in a hand.** The first rest asks for a quiet gyro, a steady force and a settled attitude (two averages of the world acceleration within StillAccel/8 of each other, so that a filter still converging after power-on isn't learned as bias), unbroken for StillTime + AvgTime. In a shaky hand (a model fitted to a recorded hand-held MPU6050: a real rotational tremor of 0.027 rad/s and an 8 Hz translational one of 0.09 m/s²), the force wasn't steady: the 8 Hz tremor took a single sample out of StillAccel of its average on about 30 % of samples, the first rest never came, the Nav held its origin, and nothing moved.
- The first rest now judges the force low-passed over AvgTime/8 against its average (`:386`, `:419`). That averages out the tremor (to about 0.05 m/s²); a stroke's acceleration passes. The trend's averages restart on the same measure, so tremor no longer resets them. After the first rest, the instantaneous steadiness is unchanged.
- `TestFirstRestInAShakyHand` (8 seeds, Mahony from power-on): no seed rested within 10 s before; all rest now. `TestPowerOnWhileTheFilterSettles` still holds.

**Inputs → outputs:** `New(Config)` returns a `Nav` at the origin. `Update(dt, q, f, w)` takes:

- `q`: a filter's attitude, `[w,x,y,z]`, sensor to world, z up (e.g. `ahrs.MahonyAHRS.Output()`); used until the first rest, and again if a stale bias sends the Nav back to it;
- `f`: the specific force, sensor frame, m/s², +g up at rest;
- `w`: the gyro rate, rad/s.

After every call the `Nav` is the body's whole motion state, for every consumer at once (the path, Pong's paddle, and the simulations that need gravity: water, fire, boids):

- `P` (m) and `V` (m/s), world frame;
- `PB` (m) and `VB` (m/s), the body (sensor) frame: `VB` is `V` rotated into the sensor frame now; `PB` adds up each sample's world displacement rotated into that sample's sensor frame. A turn in place adds nothing to `PB`; `P` rotated by the attitude now would swing with every tilt (10·sin 40° = 6.4 cm for a 40° tilt after a 10 cm move), a paddle that jumps. Pong (badge upright, IMU +Y up) follows `PB[1]` and `VB[1]`;
- `A`, the world linear acceleration: gravity rotated out and the biases removed, on every sample, at rest too (against the older bias snapshot at rest, which has none of a motion's first samples);
- `F`, the specific force in the sensor frame as given (gravity plus the body's own acceleration);
- `Q`, the attitude the Nav used: the filter's until the first rest, then its own (gyro-carried, levelled at rest);
- `Bias`, the learned world bias;
- `Still`.

For Pong, the paddle follows the badge frame's up axis, `PB[1]` and `VB[1]` (vertical position in the badge frame); `P[2]` is up in the world. Water and fire take `F` in the screen plane as their gravity.

**Seeding:** `Seed(gyroBias)` gives the Nav a startup calibration's gyro bias ([[startup-calibration]], `x/math/nav/calib`: the mean of seconds at rest), before its first rest ends. The first rest keeps it instead of learning its own from a shorter stretch, and the rest after it refines it as a long rest would (its running averages start at 4·`AvgTime`; at 0.5 s they replaced a 3 s calibration's bias with their own noise). On a noisy gyro (0.01 rad/s of noise and of bias), held in motion after a 1.5 s first rest, the attitude turned 0.036 rad in 20 s unseeded and 0.013 rad seeded with a 10 s calibration (rms of 20 draws). The accelerometer's offsets are not seeded: one pose cannot tell them from the table's tilt, and the Nav learns them across turns. **Not used on the badge yet:** on after-turn stroke set, seeding widened the reads (22 of 36 within 10 % against 26, over-reads up to 222 %) while failures dropped from 7 to 5. Seeding must not make any probe worse; r11 looks into it.

**Method (per sample):**

1. **Attitude:** the filter's q until the first rest ends; then the Nav's own, advanced by the gyro less its learned bias (first order, renormalised by one Newton step). A filter leans its attitude into any acceleration it sees: through Mahony at any KP a slow horizontal stroke reads as tilt and cancels itself, so no speed gate in 3D could work on it. The gyro does not lean.
2. **World acceleration:** r = R(att)·(f − sb) − (0, 0, G), sb the sensor-frame accelerometer offsets learned across turns (step 10).
3. **Measures:** the gyro is quiet (< `StillGyro`); no linear acceleration is left (|r − Bias| < `StillAccel`, "calm"); the specific force is steady (within `StillAccel` of its running average over `AvgTime`; the average follows the force while the gyro is busy, so steadiness counts from the end of a turn); the attitude is settled (two averages of r over `AvgTime`/2 and `AvgTime` agree within `StillAccel`/8: the filter still turning towards the true tilt at power-on parts them).
4. **First rest:** quiet, steady and settled for `StillTime`, then `AvgTime` more; the accelerometer and gyro biases are the plain means of its samples. Nothing is integrated before it; the origin holds. At its end the horizontal part of the bias levels the attitude (step 9), and the attitude passes to the gyro.
5. **Rest** (from motion): quiet, calm and the speed under `StillSpeed`, all at once and without a break for `StillTime`.
   - Low acceleration alone is not rest: 3 cm in 2 s peaks at 0.043 m/s², under any usable `StillAccel`. In an earlier version the measures were ORed and chained across a smooth stroke, so rest came mid-stroke at up to 372 mm/s and slow strokes tracked 0 % to −24 %.
   - Vertical speed always counts. Horizontal speed counts over the first `MaxGlide` of a motion: a slow stroke (30 cm in 2 s) stays under `StillAccel` for 0.36 s around its peak speed, and a gentle one throughout. Past `MaxGlide`, over a long motion, the horizontal velocity drifts by as much as a gentle stroke's speed (4 cm/s after an 8 s Pong rally) and would keep the body from ever resting. (r7 judged speed vertically only: on the filter's attitude, g·sin θ of tilt error filled the horizontal.)
   - A turned motion (step 10) waits `StillTime` + `AvgTime`/2, takes a steady force even past `StillAccel` without asking its speed, and only a constant one that explains its end.
   - **Glides:** quiet, calm and steady for `MaxGlide` is rest whatever the speed, in any direction. This bounds the drift after a wrong velocity.
6. **ZUPT:** on entering rest, velocity is zero, and the velocity left over is error that was there through the whole quiet stretch: its distance over the stretch is taken back (a real stroke's tail has slowed to nothing and is kept). Horizontally, once its speed no longer counts, over the whole stretch the body looked still. A glide's rest allows for what is left in its acceleration over the glide (a bias gone wrong builds the velocity up there): it takes back V·T − ē·T²/2, where V·T alone sent a 3 cm stroke 11 cm on. A turned motion's rest first takes the turned offsets' error out (step 10).
7. **At rest:** the world bias and the gyro bias by running averages whose time constant grows from `AvgTime` to 4·`AvgTime` over the rest. A rally without pauses integrates whatever bias error it starts with: with a 0.5 s average the Pong test ended up to 3.3 cm off, with the growing one 1.6 cm.
8. **Leaving rest:** a rotation; a strong acceleration that isn't steady; or, while calm, a shadow speed of `StillSpeed`/2 vertically or `StillSpeed` horizontally, or five standard deviations of what the noise can give the shadow if that is more: σv² = σ²·(dt·T + T²/N), σ² the noise measured in the first rest, T the shadow's age, N the first rest's samples (the bias the shadow runs against is off by σ/√N, which T multiplies). At 0.03 m/s² of noise a fixed StillSpeed/2 woke a still badge every few seconds and the glide then drifted 3 cm; the noise, left to be learned at rest from zero, read 0.6 of its value 0.6 s after calibrating and let the same happen.
   - Two shadows integrate r during the rest, each against the bias of its snapshot (one every `StillTime`). On waking, the older one, which began at least `StillTime` before, gives the velocity, the last acceleration and the distance to add, and the biases go back to its snapshot (the first samples of a motion are learned into them before the motion is seen).
   - Horizontally two axes of accelerometer noise add up: at `StillSpeed`/2, 0.03 m/s² over a 0.6 s shadow (2.3 mm/s per axis) kept waking a still badge. With an exact attitude, 3 cm in 2 s horizontally reaches `StillSpeed` a third of the way in and ends at 88 %; vertically it reaches `StillSpeed`/2 a sixth of the way in and ends at 96.2 %.
   - Woken by the gyro alone, the Nav keeps the accelerometer bias of now and takes no shadow: a turn shows from its first sample, so none of it was learned. The gyro bias goes back to the older snapshot whatever woke the body.
9. **Levelling:** at rest a horizontal force is a tilt error. Each snapshot moves the older snapshot's horizontal bias into the attitude over 4·`AvgTime` (`tilt`): the attitude leans by θ, tan θ = |m|/(G + Bias_z), and the bias gains √((G + Bias_z)² + |m|²) − (G + Bias_z) vertically, exactly (a z offset of a few tenths is common). Both snapshots move with it, so a wake that restores one finds it consistent with the attitude.
10. **Rotation in place and turns** (tilting the badge in place must not show motion; every real translation must register, and drift is acceptable when measured). The accelerometer's offsets turn with the sensor: an error δb in the sensor-frame offsets puts (R − R0)·δb into the world acceleration from the start of a motion (R0 at its rest, R now). The Nav keeps m1 = ∫(R − R0)dt and m2 = ∫∫(R − R0)dt² over each motion, so the error is m1·δb in the velocity and m2·δb in P.
    - A motion that begins with the gyro alone is a rotation: it moves nothing while it lasts and a shadow integrates it. If it shows a translation (|f − sb| moving off its rest value by more than `StillAccel` + `offsetMax`·|ĝ − ĝrest|, ĝ gravity's direction in the sensor frame; or an unsteady acceleration with the gyro quiet) the shadow is taken up, so a stroke made while turning is kept from its start and shows while it moves.
    - A motion that turned (a rotation, or |ĝ − ĝrest| of 3° or more) rests on a steady force even past `StillAccel` and without asking its speed, once `StillTime` + `AvgTime`/2 hold only noise about a constant: its halves agree (each by its own time; a sample off missed the 1 s hold after a 90° turn with uncalibrated offsets), its spread is the noise's (a stroke's start, whose halves agree, was learned as 0.13 m/s² of offsets), and the constant explains the velocity at the motion's end (m1·δb, within 2·`StillSpeed`, four standard deviations of the noise, and what offsets along the turn's axis could build: a stroke still slowing down and a glide fail this). A glide's rest waits while such a stretch runs.
    - That constant e is (R1 − R0)·δb, and (R1 − R0)ᵀ(R1 − R0) = (2 − 2·cos φ)(I − uuᵀ) for a turn by φ about u, so δb = (R1 − R0)ᵀ·e / (2 − 2·cos φ) across the axis (`turnErr`, from 0.6°). A motion that showed itself has m2·δb taken out of P and m1·δb out of its velocity: a stroke right after a 45° turn with 0.09 m/s² of offsets once read 280–330 % and then drifted 1.6 m in 30 s; now it reads within 50–150 % and holds still. A rotation's shadow, up to where its quiet stretch began and less m2·δb, is taken up into P if it stands out of four standard deviations of the noise integrated twice over the motion (σ²·dt·T³/3 per axis), of `offsetMax`·‖m2‖ after a turn under 0.6° (one that came back to where it began), and of 1 cm (`register`): a tilt in place moves nothing, a gentle stroke while the wrist wobbles shows at its rest.
    - δb then goes into sb for good from 20° (`learnTurn`; below, the division mostly amplifies the noise, and a 0.1 rad turn the gyro saw but the body did not make put 3 m/s² into sb), `Bias` takes the rest of e, and `Bias`'s horizontal part, the tilt mistaken for offsets before, levels back into the attitude. A hand pushing steadily and constantly right after a turn would be taken for the turn's leftover.
11. **Stale bias:** quiet and steady with a strong acceleration (not calm) for `StillTime` + `AvgTime` is no hand but a bias gone stale (a 0.4 m/s² step at rest otherwise ran 5 m in 5 s; after a turn the turned rest, 0.55 s, comes first). The attitude goes back to the filter's, the bias is learned again as a first rest, and the position goes back to where that stretch began.
12. **Motion:** the acceleration integrated is r − Bias; velocity and position both by the trapezoid; velocity times (1 − `Damping`·dt). The samples are of a smooth motion: taking each acceleration for the whole step before it adds half a step of it to the velocity (1 cm/s on a 2 m/s² Pong stroke). (r9's allowance, shrinking up to `StillAccel` out of the acceleration while turning, is gone: it bent the turn's error out of the linear form step 10 corrects, and tilts read up to 1.2 cm.)

**Numbers:** float32, matching the badge's Mahony filter and `pdr`. Three quaternion rotations (into the world, and back into the body for `PB` and `VB`), one quaternion step for the gyro, a rotation matrix and 18 multiply-adds for the turn's error terms: about a hundred operations per sample; one square root per window roll (levelling), none per sample. Non-finite samples and a non-positive dt are ignored.

**Limits:**

- Accelerometer noise random-walks the position: at about an MPU6050's 0.02 m/s² rms, a few millimetres per second of motion. Rests stop the velocity, not that offset. In the Pong test the end was up to 1.6 cm off vertically after 6 s of strokes; each stroke stays right.
- Waving without a pause has no rest to correct it (10 cm strokes of 0.5 s, 0.02 m/s² noise, five draws): off by up to 4 mm after 2 s, 2 cm after 4 s, 11 cm after 9.5 s.
- Gyro noise random-walks the attitude between rests, and 0.17 m/s² of horizontal acceleration leaks in per degree. The tests take 0.001–0.002 rad/s (an MPU6050 at 44 Hz shows 0.0006); a second harness also at 0.005, with no change.
- Tilting a badge in place, 60 cases (15°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90° over 1, 0.5 and 0.2 s, upright about the screen normal and the screen's x, flat about x and y) at an MPU6050's noise (accelerometer 0.03 m/s² Gaussian, gyro 0.002 rad/s, gyro bias 0.005 rad/s), offsets of 0.09, 0.7 and 1.05 m/s², 30 seeds from each of six ranges, and at gyro bias 0.02 rad/s: 0.0 mm in all 37,800 runs, every run at rest after. a second harness (flat about (1,1,0), gyro noise up to 0.005): 60/60 at 0.0 cm. r8 strayed up to 3 cm here, r5 58 cm, r3 2–75 cm.
- Strokes that never show themselves while they move (3–5 cm over 1–1.5 s while the wrist turns, 3–10 cm over 1.5–2 s right after a turn) jump into place at the rest after them, not while they move.
- **Known failures (ER-067; the next revision's targets):**
  - 3 cm over 1.5 s: of 144 stroke runs, 5 do not register (one while the wrist wobbles at 0.1 rad/s, three at 1 rad/s, one right after a 45° turn; exactly 0 %) and 3 read the wrong way (−109 % wobbling at 0.1 rad/s; −736 % and −781 % right after a 45° turn). Every stroke of 5 cm or more, and every 3 cm stroke of 1 s or less, registers. These were measured as mostly design (the registration threshold and how a turn's offset error is attributed), not the sensor: the physical part is a standard deviation of about 23 % at an MPU6050's noise (10 % and 5 % at half and a quarter of it). In my 60 runs of 3 cm right after a turn, reads ran from −250 % to 170 %, and what a misread rest leaves drifted up to 20 cm over the next 10 s, with 24 wakes, before it rested for good.
  - 3 cm in 2 s with no turn: one draw reads −67 % at the stroke's end, −204 % a second later and −39 % at rest; noisy horizontal grid reads −302 % 1.5 s after, still gliding (the noise leaves up to 2 cm/s, above `StillSpeed`, so rest waits for `MaxGlide`). A glide after a turn rests at once (its distance is lost).
  - Slow 2 s strokes can jump back at their late rest, about 1 s after the stroke (one of three draws each): 10 cm in 2 s reads 106–110 % live, then 48 %; 5 cm, 95 % then 54 %.
  - A turn followed at once by a 10 cm stroke peaks at 21.6 cm before the rest corrects it to 9.9 cm.
- Horizontal strokes: 3 cm in 2 s ends at 88 % with an exact attitude (the wake-up at `StillSpeed`); through Mahony with a noisy sensor, 41 of 42 horizontal strokes (3–30 cm over 0.3–2 s) end within 10 % and at rest, the worst at 111 %, up to 6.4 cm across. Vertically 36 of 42 with noise (3 mm is 10 % of 3 cm), all 42 with an ideal sensor.
- A long horizontal glide is stopped after `MaxGlide`, a long motion's horizontal drift taken back over the stretch it looked still.
- Sustained motion without rest drifts quadratically. `Damping` bounds it at the cost of short motions.
- No magnetometer: yaw, and with it the path's heading, is relative and drifts with the gyro bias.
