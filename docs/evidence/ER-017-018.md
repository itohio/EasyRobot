# ER-017 / ER-018 — IK robustness, leg helpers, gait scheduler, footholds and walk glue

**Result:** both tickets' acceptance items pass (M-a stage 1, host simulation). A hexapod walks a tripod gait for 15 s in simulation — 10 s at 0.1 m/s while turning at 0.3 rad/s, then 5 s turning in place. Stance feet stay fixed in the world (max drift 9.5e-8 m), every target is reachable (min margin 9.1 mm), every joint stays inside its limits, and one tick allocates nothing. Every fix started from a test that failed first, and the failing output is recorded. Three internal review rounds plus the roster review (`docs/review/R-ER-017-018.md`, approve with fixes); all its majors and minors are fixed test-first, nothing deferred except the items in §7 (§4). Verify accepted. Uncommitted; the commits wait for Leader's go.

**Open / decisions:**
- Joint speed is reported, not limited: the hexapod sim peaks at 8.0 rad/s against a 5.24 rad/s (300°/s) servo. The ticket asked for position limits only. TODO in [[gait-foothold-planner]].
- **VAJ1D now computes in float64 internally** (fix for float32 rounding bias on very long brakes). The repo convention is float32 and the manipulator node is a SAMD21 without FPU — owner/Leader to accept, or ask for a float32 variant with the long-brake case documented as a limit ([[vaj1d-jerk-limited-profile]]).
- When no scaled twist keeps the reach margin and stopping is unsafe, the walker now **slows along its previous twist** (smallest feasible fraction) instead of holding it. It travels least after a stop command, but a quadruped without slew limits takes up to 45 ticks to stop instead of 12 (hexapod 9–15 ticks). Owner to accept, or ask for an explicit stop path ([[gait-foothold-planner]]).
- MCU cost of `Walker.Step` is unmeasured (host: 45 µs unclipped, 0.41 ms mean under random commands).
