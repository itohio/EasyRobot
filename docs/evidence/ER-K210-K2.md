# ER-CAM-steps K2 — QQVGA Y8 over UART at 115200: bars, then the room (full field vs crop)

Sensor: **OV2640 at SCCB 0x30**. Owner (via Leader, 2026-11-09): "the full field of view scaled down as well as a windowed crop", one frame each, side by side.

## Fresh frames first (cameras moved)

- `fresh/rgb565-qvga/{1,3,4}.png` — QVGA RGB565 (`images=3 crc_errors=3 dropped=2`)
- `fresh/y8-qvga/{0,3,7}.png` — QVGA Y8 (`images=3 crc_errors=3 dropped=4 lost=1`)
- `fresh/y8-qvga-2/0.png` — QVGA Y8, taken after K2 (`images=1 crc_errors=0`): **the current view**.

The first two sets still showed the [[ER-K210-M4]] scene (shelves left, wall with socket right). By the time K2 ran the view had changed (shelves top centre, a large object lower left, desk and a lit strip bottom), and `fresh/y8-qvga-2` confirms it. So the camera was moved during this session, after the first fresh capture.

## Check (written before the run)

Firmware `drivers/unitv/examples/frame`, QQVGA (160×120) Y8, 115200 baud, unpaced. Each run: `camframe -n 3`. Pass = all three hold:
1. **Bars** (`pattern=1`): 3 images, `crc_errors=0 dropped=0`. The PNG shows 8 vertical bars whose mean interior luma falls left to right (white > yellow > cyan > green > magenta > red > blue > black).
2. **Full field** (`crop=0`): 3 images, `crc_errors=0 dropped=0`, recognisable room PNG.
3. **Crop** (`crop=1`): 3 images, `crc_errors=0 dropped=0`. The PNG shows the centre of the full-field view magnified ~2.5× (the crop is 160 of the CIF frame's 400 sensor columns).

## Result: **pass** (full field needed a second run)

Builds: `tinygo build -target=drivers/unitv/target/unitv.json -ldflags "-X main.mode=y8-qqvga -X main.baud=115200 -X main.burst=0 -X main.<pattern=1|crop=0|crop=1>" ./drivers/unitv/examples/frame`. Capture: `camframe -port /dev/cu.usbserial-6D52BBCC47 -baud 115200 -n 3 -out k2/<run> -raw logs/k2-<run>.raw`.

| Run | camframe summary | Check |
|---|---|---|
| bars | `images=3 saved=3 fps=0.57 crc_errors=0 dropped=0 lost=0` | pass, measured on frame 2 (`k2/bars/2.png`); frame 0 is the first after `Open`, taken before exposure settles, and is saturated (verify ER-CAM-BATCH-2): interior mean luma per bar 215.6, 196.8, 161.2, 150.1, 69.9, 59.7, 27.1, 15.1 (strictly falling) |
| full field, run 1 | `images=3 saved=3 fps=0.27 crc_errors=2 dropped=2 lost=1` | **fail** |
| full field, run 2 | `images=3 saved=3 fps=0.58 crc_errors=0 dropped=0 lost=0` | pass |
| crop | `images=3 saved=3 fps=0.58 crc_errors=0 dropped=0 lost=0` | pass |

- **Run 1's losses were on the link, at 115200.** In `logs/k2-full.raw`, chunk 1 of image 1 runs 6,068 bytes to the next magic (the next chunk's header and ~2 KB lost); chunk 1 of image 2 has 3,009 of 4,096 bytes before the next magic; image 3 never arrived. The device printed no capture/write error. So 115200 is **not** loss-free on this FT232BM/macOS path, only much better than 1.5 Mbaud. Cause unknown, open for K5.
- **Frame time:** 1.71–1.74 s per image (19.2 KB at 115200; device timestamps 0 → 1,710,788 → 3,449,376 µs).
